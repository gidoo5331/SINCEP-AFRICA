About the project

One network, preparing together

Epidemics don't respect borders, and neither can the response. SINCEP-Africa connects universities, research institutes and health services across sub-Saharan Africa with partners in Europe and North America, so that the region has the people, research sites and data it needs to act quickly when an outbreak starts.

Over five years the consortium will train health experts, strengthen research and clinical trial sites, and build shared systems for disease surveillance. The work is coordinated by the EDCTP Association, with INDEPTH Network as scientific lead.

Key insights

How the network breaks down

Five patterns that shape how SINCEP-Africa is built.

🏛️

Universities dominate

12 of 21 partners (57%) are universities, balanced by 7 research institutes and 2 cross-border networks.

🌍

Strong West African core

Ghana (4), Burkina Faso (2), Nigeria, Senegal, Mali, and Ivory Coast (1 each) make up 10 of 16 SSA partners.

🔗

European bridge partners

LSTM (UK), UNAV (Spain), PANTHER (France), and EDCTP (Netherlands) anchor the European side.

📍

Broad East African reach

Ethiopia, Tanzania, Uganda, and Kenya provide coverage across universities and research institutes.

🚩

Southern & Central Africa included

Fundação Manhiça (Mozambique) and UNIKIN (DRC) round out a truly pan-African consortium.
