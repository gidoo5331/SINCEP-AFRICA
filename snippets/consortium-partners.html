Role Leads

What do the roles mean?

Coordinator
Manages the project on behalf of the consortium and is the main contact with the funder, Global Health EDCTP3.
Scientific lead
Sets the project's scientific direction and brings together the network of African research sites.
Beneficiary
A partner organisation that receives funding to carry out project work. Some beneficiaries also lead one of the nine work areas.
