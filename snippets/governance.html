Consortium Bodies

View Organogram

The governance structure of the consortium comprises five bodies, each with distinct roles and responsibilities.

  • Project Steering Committee (PSC)

    The decision‑making body of the consortium, made up of the leader of each partner beneficiary.

  • Coordinator

    Legal intermediary between the Parties and the Granting Authority. Co-chairs PSC meetings.

  • Scientific Leadership

    Promotes networking and partnership, and provides technical oversight of implementation. Co-chairs PSC meetings.

  • Work Package Leaders Group (WPLG)

    An assessment group without formal decision‑making power, reviewing project implementation.

  • Scientific Advisory Board (SAB)

    Appointed and steered by the PSC to assist and facilitate its decisions.

    Scientific Advisory Board

    Nine‑member board — appointed by the PSC

    The Scientific Advisory Board (SAB) will be appointed and steered by the Project Steering Committee. The SAB shall assist and facilitate the decisions made by the PSC.

    The Scientific Advisory Board will contribute its expertise and knowledge of the health and scientific ecosystem to SINCEP-Africa in order to facilitate the proper coordination of its research and interventions activities, but above all to ensure that they are integrated into the health systems of Africa.

    The Scientific Advisory Board's contribution ranges from recommendations and advice on operational projects to scientific and/or operational research aimed at providing solutions to people's health problems, thereby helping to reduce morbidity and mortality in Africa.

    In addition, the Scientific Advisory Board will facilitate connection and networking with African and international institutions, the various ministries of health and industry, thereby contributing to a rapid and appropriate response to the various health emergencies.
